NIS2 Directive National Transposition 27 Member States

Every country transposes differently.

Twenty-seven jurisdictions, one platform.

While Directive 2022/2555 sets the framework, each EU member state transposes NIS2 into national law differently. Transposition laws vary in their scope extensions, penalty levels, sector definitions, entity classification thresholds, and reporting timelines. Konforme tracks each country’s specific rules so your compliance posture reflects the law that actually applies to you.
